## Strategy

From Pin #38 onward, prioritise:

- Specific Okanagan destinations, not the valley in general
- Specific experiences and long-tail search topics
- Individual regions and towns
- Seasonal activities and weekend-trip intent
- Topics people actually search on Pinterest
- Content that can send a reader to a relevant Okanagan Roam page

Avoid repeating broad topics — "Okanagan Travel", "Things to Do in the Okanagan",
"Fall in the Okanagan" — unless the search intent is genuinely different.

### Rotation rules

Rotate **destination**, **board**, **content category** and **visual concept**
across every batch. No board should carry a whole batch; no destination should
dominate one.

**Content categories:** fall travel · weekend getaways · wineries · wine tasting ·
food & drink · beaches · lakes · hiking · cycling · golf · scenic viewpoints ·
scenic drives · hidden gems · farm experiences · apple picking · pumpkin patches ·
corn mazes · harvest · events · family activities · outdoor adventures · camping ·
nature & wildlife · winter (as the season approaches) · local experiences ·
road trips · itineraries · destination guides

**Visual concepts:** landscape · vineyard · lakeside · orchard · town · food ·
wine · hiking · golf · beach · viewpoint · road trip · farm · adventure.
Vary time of day: morning · afternoon · golden hour · overcast · bright autumn ·
close-up detail · wide landscape.
